Attributes:

Student: S_Phone_number, S_Address
Subjects: Sub_name, Sub_description.
Lecturer: L_phonenumber

relationships: 

Relationship between Student and Enrolment is one to many as a student can have multiple enrolments, while each enrolment belongs to a student.

The relationship between Lecturer and Subjects is many to many because a lecturer can teach multiple subjects, and a subject can be taught by multiple lecturers.

the relationship between Lecture and Subjects is many to one because a subject can have multiple lectures and each lecture is associated with one subject.
